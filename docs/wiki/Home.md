# Azeroth Threatwarden

*Italiano: [[Italiano|Home-it]]*

![Azeroth Threatwarden logo](../../assets/logo-source.webp)

**Know your threat. Understand your mistakes. Prevent aggro loss.** Azeroth Threatwarden is a threat monitor, threat
coach and combat analysis addon for World of Warcraft: Forever.

> **Status: early development, nothing to install yet.** So far the addon only has its window, its commands and its minimap button: it reads no threat yet. The first step checks in the real client which threat data the
> game gives an addon. The first version, `v0.1.0`, comes after milestone M1; the MVP (threat monitor, alerts, Coach,
> recording and combat report) is `v0.4.0`. This manual grows with each version.

## What it will do

- **Threat Monitor**: every group member's threat on your target, the tank, how close each one is to pulling aggro,
  and a state: *Safe*, *Warning*, *Danger* or *Unknown*.
- **Alerts and Coach**: a warning before you overtake the tank, and short advice with its reason.
- **Combat Report, Timeline and Replay**: every fight recorded while it happens, to look at afterwards.
- **Honest data**: the addon always says whether a number is read from the game, calculated, estimated or predicted,
  and shows *Unknown* instead of guessing.
