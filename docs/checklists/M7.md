# M7 in-game check: multi-target and Dungeon Focus

The offline tests prove that the code agrees with our assumptions about the client; this checklist proves that the
client agrees with the code. It checks the exit criterion of [milestone M7](../roadmap/M7-multi-target.md) (*in a
dungeon pull, the focus list shows the mobs at risk and degrades cleanly on mobs that cannot be queried*). It is **not
done yet**: nobody has run the addon on WoW Forever, and M7 was built before the [M0 probe](../spike/README.md) was run,
on a **conditional go** ([SPEC-07 §0](../specs/SPEC-07-multi-target-heatmap-profiles.md#0-il-gate-di-m7-go-condizionato),
Italian).

**Run the probe first if you can**, or in the same pulls: checks **P5** (which units and mobs answer) and **P6** (the
threat events) decide how much of this milestone can work at all. The steps marked **[P5]**, **[P6]**, **[P9]** (GUIDs)
and **[P10]** (cost) also answer those checks; note their results with the probe's. The manual pages
[Dungeon Focus](../wiki/Dungeon-Focus.md) and [Alerts and Coach](../wiki/Alerts-and-Coach.md#alerts-about-other-mobs)
describe what you should see; the rules are in
[SPEC-07 §1–§2](../specs/SPEC-07-multi-target-heatmap-profiles.md) (Italian).

Report the results with the [*In-game test report* form](https://github.com/Finland6738/azeroth-threatwarden/issues/new?template=ingame_test.yml)
(*Check*: for example `M7 step 4.2`) and note them in the table [at the end](#result). One report for everything that
passed is enough; open one per thing that did not.

**A reduced view is not always a bug.** *Focus and boss only*, *Only your target can be read here*, *Threat cannot be
read here*, a mob marked unknown with a reason, or *state only* rows are the right answer when the client gives no more.
What must never happen: an invented number or a 0 standing for "unknown", *All safe* while some mob cannot be read, the
same mob listed twice, a pinned mob changing your game target, an alert or any message in chat, a Lua error or a blocked
action.

## What you need

- WoW Forever; a **party** with a tank and at least one damage dealer (you are one of them; run the alert steps once
  as the damage dealer or healer and once as the tank if you can), a **dungeon** with pulls of **3 to 6 mobs** and a
  boss, and a **raid** if you can, ideally 40 players.
- The game's **enemy nameplates** key, and a **focus** (`/focus` on a mob, `/clearfocus` to remove it).
- On the PC, as in the [M2 checklist](M2.md#what-you-need), a development copy of an up-to-date `main` (or the released
  `v0.7.0`).
- Lua errors visible: `/console scriptErrors 1`, or BugSack.
- **Back up your saved data first**, with the game closed. Copy both files somewhere else:
  - `<WoW folder>/_forever_/WTF/Account/<account>/SavedVariables/AzerothThreatwarden.lua` (settings and recordings);
  - `<WoW folder>/_forever_/WTF/Account/<account>/<realm>/<character>/SavedVariables/AzerothThreatwarden.lua` (window
    place and size), for each character you use.

  A copy of `main` raises the account data to **schema 6** (new settings only; recordings are not changed): if you go
  back to `v0.6.0` afterwards, that version shows it **read-only** until you restore your copy. The files hold the names
  of your companions: do not attach them to an issue.

Then install:

```bash
python -I -X utf8 tools/fetch_libs.py
```

```bash
python -I -X utf8 tools/install_addon.py
```

- **Before turning the switch on**, do step 1.1 (not needed from `v0.7.0`).
- **Turn the switch on** (not needed from `v0.7.0`, where it is on). With the game closed, open
  `<WoW folder>/_forever_/Interface/AddOns/AzerothThreatwarden/Core/Features.lua`, change `multitarget = false` to
  `multitarget = true`, save, and start the game. Edit only the installed copy (installing again undoes it, so repeat
  it after every install); never commit that change. `/atw debug` *Features* must list `multitarget` after `on:`, and
  the *Mobs* section must start with `switch on, setting on`.
- Settings at their defaults (*Reset settings*): *Track other mobs* on, *Mobs followed* **8**, *Use enemy nameplates*
  on, *Alerts about other mobs* off, thresholds **82 % / 91 %**.

`/atw debug` (section **Mobs**) has switches, reach, counts and the reads by source only, no names and no GUIDs, so its
text is safe to paste. Its counts add up **since the last login or `/reload`**: `/reload` before a pull whose numbers you
want alone. Screenshots show names: replace them with `<player>`.

## 1. Switch off, then on

1. **With the switch off** (skip from `v0.7.0`), in a pull of several mobs: no pull bar, no *Dungeon Focus* card, no
   *Alerts about other mobs* switch, no pin; `/atw focus` answers *This arrives in v0.7.0.*; the monitor, alerts and
   recording work as before; the *Mobs* section says `switch off`.
2. **With the switch on**: no Lua error and no chat message at login; nothing opens by itself; the *Dungeon Focus* card
   is after *Monitor*, the *Alerts about other mobs* switch is in the *Alerts* card (off); `/atw help` lists `focus`.
3. Out of combat, `/atw focus` opens the Monitor tab and says *No other mobs to show right now.*; `/atw demo` shows
   *Pull · 6* and the invented list (loose, close, no tank, secret and gone mobs), and a mob cannot be pinned there.

## 2. A dungeon pull of 3–6 mobs, nameplates shown (exit criterion) **[P5]**

Window open on the Monitor tab, enemy nameplates **shown**, no focus. `/reload`, then pull.

1. The **pull bar** appears under the header within about a second and disappears after the fight. The count matches the
   mobs fighting you (at most *Mobs followed*, the monitored one included); no mob twice, no mob that is not fighting,
   dead mobs leave at once.
2. Click the bar (and try `/atw focus`): the **list** takes the place of the threat list, one line per mob, the
   monitored one ringed. Click again (or type it again): the threat list is back.
3. Compare the holder, the closest unit and the percentage with what happens (target a mob and check it against the
   monitor): they must agree. Values come with their age and category in the tooltip.
4. The order: Danger first, unknown last; it changes at most once a second, and at once when a mob enters Danger. Does it
   feel steady enough to click?
5. Where mobs are found: note in the tooltips the sources you see (*Nameplate*, *Group target*, *Boss*, *Your target*).
6. A nameplate that leaves the screen and comes back (turn the camera, walk away): the mob keeps its line, *No unit*
   when nothing names it, and it comes back as the same mob. Note whether it was found again without a nameplate or a
   member targeting it (*Found again by its identity*) **[P9]**.
7. Do new mobs (adds, a second pack) appear, and how quickly: under a second, a few seconds, never **[P5, P6]**?
8. After the fight copy `/atw debug` *Mobs*: reach, identity, `reads by source (values/state/none)` and `events:`
   **[P5, P6]**.

## 3. Nameplates hidden, and with a focus **[P5]**

1. Same kind of pull with the enemy **nameplates hidden**: only the mobs of your focus, the bosses and the group's
   targets appear. A mob nobody targets does not. The tooltip of a reduced view reminds you of the nameplate key. The
   addon must not have turned nameplates on.
2. With nameplates shown but *Use enemy nameplates* off: the same result.
3. Set a **focus** on a mob that nobody targets, nameplates hidden: it appears, labelled *Focus*, with values; change
   and clear the focus during the fight.
4. On a **boss** with adds: do `boss1`–`boss5` answer (label *Boss*)?
5. *Mobs followed* at **2** with a bigger pull: only two mobs, the monitored one among them; at **12** with the largest
   pull you can make: the extra ones are summed in *+N more* if the window is short.
6. *Track other mobs* off: no bar, nothing read (`/atw debug` *Mobs* `setting off`), `/atw focus` says *Other mobs are
   off in Settings.*

## 4. A loose mob, and one the tank has not picked up

Agree with the tank before the pull.

1. A damage dealer takes one mob off the tank (not your monitored target): the bar says **On *name*** and that mob is
   at the top of the list with a warning icon on its holder, within a couple of seconds. The tank takes it back: it
   returns to *All safe* or lower.
2. Raise your threat on another mob without taking it: **Close**, then **Getting close** below 91 %.
3. The tank leaves one mob alone while a damage dealer or healer has it: first *On name*; a mob with no tank on its list,
   held by someone outside your group (a pet, a guardian, an NPC), says **No tank**. If you cannot make this, write
   *skipped*.
4. With every mob safely on the tank: *All safe*. If a mob is unreadable the bar says *All safe · N unknown*, never just
   *All safe*.
5. Without any tank role (a group with no tank, or solo with a pet): there is no *loose* or *No tank*, only *Close* and
   *Getting close*. Note whether that is what you expect.

## 5. Reach in a party, a dungeon and a raid; secret mobs **[P5, P9]**

For each context you have (open world in a party, dungeon trash, dungeon boss, raid), after one pull:

1. Note the **reach**: the list's footer (*Reach: all · 6 of 8 · 0.4 s ago*) and the bar when reduced (*Focus and boss
   only*, *Only your target can be read here*, *Threat cannot be read here*). The tooltip gives a reason (identity
   hidden, mobs not told apart, secret threat, nameplates off, no answer). Does the reason match what you see in the
   game?
2. A single-mob fight in a group must **not** show a reduced bar (nor a bar at all).
3. **Secret** mobs (if the client hides anything): the line is unknown, says *Secret value*, no number anywhere on the
   line; the tooltip may show the last reading with its age; *state only* rows show no percentage.
4. A mob whose line says *Stale* or *Waiting*: how often, and for how long? A stale line must not keep its numbers.
5. Copy `/atw debug` *Mobs* for each context.

## 6. Following a mob: click, new target, header pin

1. In a pull, click a mob that is not your target: the list closes, the monitor's header and rows are that mob's, the
   **pin** shows next to its name, and **your game target does not change**. Was the click quick enough to use in a
   fight?
2. Check the monitor values of the pinned mob; the alerts and the recording follow it as they follow a monitored mob.
3. Release it, each way, in separate tries: a click on the header **pin**; a click on its line again; choosing a **new
   enemy target** (the monitor goes to it); the mob's **death**; moving far enough that nothing names it; the **end of
   the fight**. Choosing a friendly target keeps the pin.
4. Pin a mob through its nameplate and make the nameplate move to another mob (camera): the monitor stays on the pinned
   mob, never on the other.
5. A mob that cannot be pinned says why in its tooltip (*Cannot be followed: …*), and clicking does nothing wrong.

## 7. Alerts about other mobs

Turn on **Alerts about other mobs** in the *Alerts* card (leave *Alerts about others* at **When I tank**, sound on for
one try).

1. **Off** first: pull and make a mob loose: no alert about it, only the bar (and the alerts of your monitored mob).
2. On, **as the tank**: *A mob is on Name*, *Name is about to pull a mob*, *A mob is not on the tank*, once per change,
   not repeated while nothing changes, and the same mob waits the cooldown. At most one alert about others every 3 s,
   the most serious first.
3. On, as a **damage dealer or healer**: *Your threat on another mob is very high* once when you go close to taking, or
   take, a mob that is not your target. As the tank it never appears. With *When I tank* the alerts about others do not
   reach you.
4. Nothing for a mob that gets safer or cannot be read, nothing about your old target when you switch targets, and no
   burst when you switch the setting on during a fight.
5. **Sound**: one for Danger, none for *not on the tank*; with the window **closed** the alerts come only while the sound
   is on. Muting (`/atw mute`) and a muted unit apply.
6. After the fight the report and the replay hold the monitored mob only: no alert about other mobs recorded; nothing in
   any chat. `/atw debug` *Mobs* ends with `alerts about other mobs: on, N raised`.
7. **Does it help a tank, or is it noise?** Note how many alerts a normal pull raises (Q26).

## 8. Performance with 40 nameplates **[P10]**

In a raid (or the largest crowd of mobs you can gather), enemy nameplates shown:

1. FPS with the addon **disabled**, then enabled with the window closed, then with the pull bar, then with the list open,
   in comparable pulls. Note the numbers and the number of nameplates.
2. Any hitch when a pack appears or dies? Compare with *Track other mobs* off.
3. After a long fight copy `/atw debug` *Mobs* (*read(s) last tick, per tick*; at most 40 per tick) and, if you use an
   addon that shows it, the memory of the addon at the start and end.
4. In a raid, are the values still fresh enough to act on (age in the footer and tooltips)?

## 9. Small sizes and Italian

1. The window at its smallest size, a middle one and the largest; window scale 50 %, 100 %, 200 %; UI scale 0.64 and
   1.0; **English and Italian**.
2. The bar and the list stay readable **in combat**: the state word and icon never cut, names shortened or dropped
   first, the footer and *+N more* understandable. The smallest window shows a few lines and *+N more*.
3. In Italian: *Tutto al sicuro*, *Su Nome*, *Vicino*, *Senza tank*, the reach sentences and the card *Dungeon Focus*
   fit; the *Avvisi sugli altri nemici* switch and its note, and the four alert messages, read well.
4. In the **three themes**: an unknown mob never looks like a safe one or a number.
5. Tooltips of the bar and of a line fit on screen.

## What to report if something does not work

| Symptom | What to send |
|---|---|
| Lua error or blocked action | the error text, what you did, `/atw debug` |
| Mob missing, listed twice or wrong | context, nameplates on or off, what you saw, `/atw debug` *Mobs* |
| Wrong holder, state or number | context, the line's tooltip text (names hidden), the monitor's values, `/atw debug` |
| Wrong reach or reason | context, the bar's tooltip, `/atw debug` *Mobs* |
| Pin or release wrong | which way you released, what happened, `/atw debug` |
| Wrong or repeated alert | role, settings of the *Alerts* card, the text, `/atw debug` |
| Freeze or low FPS | step 8 numbers, group size, number of nameplates, other addons |
| Unreadable or cut text | screenshot (names hidden), window size, scale, language, theme |
