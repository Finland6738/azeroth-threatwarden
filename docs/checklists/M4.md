# M4 in-game check: recording, combat report and the MVP

The offline tests prove that the code agrees with our assumptions about the client; this checklist proves that the
client agrees with the code. It checks the exit criterion of [milestone M4](../roadmap/M4-recording-report.md) (*fights
become separate sessions that survive `/reload`; the report of each shows duration, threshold crossings, time in WARNING
and DANGER and tank changes, and says "Insufficient data" where it must; storage limits hold*) and it ends with the
[MVP check](#12-mvp-check). It is **not done yet**: nobody has run the addon on WoW Forever. The recorder, the storage
and the report were written before the [M0 probe](../spike/README.md) was run, on assumptions.

**They stand on the monitor.** A recording stores only what the monitor read, so a wrong value or state there is a wrong
report here. Run the probe, the [M2](M2.md) and the [M3](M3.md) checklists first or in the same sessions. Run M2 and
M3 **with *Record fights* off** (Settings, *Recording* card): with recording on, threat is also read while the window
is closed, so their "nothing is read" steps do not apply. The [manual page](../wiki/Combat-Reports.md) describes what you should see; the
rules are in [SPEC-05](../specs/SPEC-05-recording-report-replay.md) (Italian).

Report the results with the [*In-game test report* form](https://github.com/Finland6738/azeroth-threatwarden/issues/new?template=ingame_test.yml)
(*Check*: for example `M4 step 2.3`) and note them in the table [at the end](#result). One report for everything that
passed is enough; open one per thing that did not.

**A missing number is not always a bug.** *Not available*, *Insufficient data* or *at least …* with a reason is the
right answer when the client did not give the data. What must never happen: an invented number, a report that names a
cause or an ability, a recording lost by a `/reload` after the fight, a message in any chat, or a Lua error.

## What you need

- WoW Forever; a **party** with a tank and a damage dealer, a **dungeon** (trash and a boss) and, if you can, a
  **raid**; an enemy with many hit points or a training dummy.
- On the PC, as in the [M2 checklist](M2.md#what-you-need), a development copy of an up-to-date `main` (or the released
  `v0.4.0`):

```bash
python -I -X utf8 tools/fetch_libs.py
```

```bash
python -I -X utf8 tools/install_addon.py
```

- Lua errors visible: `/console scriptErrors 1`, or BugSack.
- **Back up your saved data first.** The file is
  `<WoW folder>/_forever_/WTF/Account/<account>/SavedVariables/AzerothThreatwarden.lua`; copy it somewhere else with
  the game closed. A copy of `main` raises the saved data to schema 5: if you go back to `v0.3.0` afterwards, that
  version shows it **read-only** until you restore your copy. The file also holds the names of your companions: do not
  attach it to an issue.
- **Switches**: since `v0.4.0` recording and report are on; nothing to edit. `/atw debug` *Features* must list
  `recording` and `reports` after `on:`.
- Settings at their defaults (*Reset settings*): *Record fights* on, *When* **In a group or instance**, rate **2 Hz**,
  **25** recordings, **2 MB**, *Notice after a fight* **off**.
- A recording closes **5 s after the fight ends**: wait that long before looking at the list.

`/atw debug` (section **Recording**) has counts only, no names, so its text is safe to paste. Screenshots show names:
replace them with `<player>`.

## 1. Loading, the tab and the card

Out of combat.

1. If you had saved data from `v0.3.0`: thresholds you never touched (80 % / 95 %) are now **82 % / 91 %**; thresholds
   you had chosen yourself are unchanged. No other setting changed.
2. No Lua error and no chat message at login. The window has the **Reports** tab; `/atw reports` opens it.
3. With no recordings it says *No recordings yet* and why: in the open world alone, *Recording only in a group or an
   instance*; with *Record fights* off, *Recording is off.*
4. `/atw settings`: the **Recording** card is below *Alerts* and above *Reset*, nothing overlaps or is cut, in English
   and in Italian. It says the recordings keep the names of your companions.
5. Change every control; `/reload`: all kept. The sliders save when you release them. *Reset settings* puts them back
   and **does not** delete recordings.
6. `/atw demo`, let it play, stop it: **no recording** was made.

## 2. A fight in a party (exit criterion)

The tank pulls; you go above Warning and Danger at least once; take the aggro once and let the tank taunt back.

1. While fighting, the tab lists the fight with the badge *Recording* and *In progress*; it cannot be opened or deleted.
   The window closed does not stop it: do one fight with the window closed.
2. After the fight, one recording: date and time (your local time?), zone, main enemy, length, your worst state.
3. Click it: a *Fight* card and one card per member. Check against what you saw: **duration**; **tanks observed**;
   **tank changes** (swap the mob between two tanks if you have them); **aggro losses** (who, when, *+N points in the
   3 s before* where there are values); **rises to** and **time in** Warning and Danger (thresholds 82 / 91 written
   under them); **highest recorded** percentage; **data quality**; **deaths**; **alerts** (with muted ones).
4. Hold the mouse over several metrics: category (*detected*, *calculated*, *estimated*, *unknown*) and, where a value
   is missing, the reason. A value shown *at least …* says part of the time was unknown.
5. No text says **why** something happened or names an ability or a class.
6. Count your own threshold crossings during one slow fight (hover around 82 % and 91 %) and compare.
7. Make a member **die**, let the tank die once, kill the mob: the deaths appear with their times (probe P7). With none,
   it says *No death recorded*, never "no deaths". Note any death that is missing or doubled.
8. A member you only saw for a moment (joined late, left the threat list at once) shows *Insufficient data*.
9. Reload (`/reload`) after the fight: the same list and the same report.
10. Nothing was posted in any chat.

## 3. Pulls, short fights and *When*

1. Pull a second enemy **less than 5 s** after the first dies: **one** recording, with *2 pulls* and *+1 other enemy*.
2. Pull again **more than 5 s** later (wait 10 s): a **second** recording.
3. A fight of **less than 3 s** (one hit on a weak enemy): no recording; `/atw debug` counts it as *dropped*.
4. Solo, outside instances, with *In a group or instance*: nothing recorded, and `/atw debug` says
   `last fight not recorded because: when`. Choose **Always**: it is recorded. Choose **In instances** in a party in the
   open world: not recorded.
5. *Record fights* off during a fight: the recording closes with what it had. Join or leave a group during a fight: the
   recording does not start or stop because of it.
6. Boss in a dungeon or raid (probe P7): the report mentions the encounter and whether it was won or lost.

## 4. Dungeon and raid: values, state only, nothing readable

Do 2.1–2.5 on trash and on a boss in a **dungeon**, then in a **raid**. Note the monitor's pill in each context
(M2 checklist, step 5), then check the report:

1. **Values**: percentages for everyone readable; rows without numbers are counted in *data quality*.
2. **State only**: percentages *Not available*, rises and times follow the *game states* (written under them), aggro
   losses without points.
3. **Nothing readable**: duration, enemy, deaths and data quality are there (*Unknown 100 %* with a reason such as
   *secret*); every threat metric is *Not available* with the reason; the report does not look broken.
4. A mode that changes during the fight gives lower bounds and *Game states and thresholds*, not mixed-up numbers.
5. Hidden names show as *Party 2*, *Raid 13* or *Pet*, never empty; the same person listed twice (a GUID that became
   readable half way) is worth reporting (probe P9).
6. A big raid pull: any limit reached (*Limit reached* badge, *Samples stopped at …*)? Copy `/atw debug`.
7. No "action blocked" or "interface action failed".

## 5. Reload, logout and disconnection

1. `/reload` **in the middle** of a fight, keep fighting, finish. The list has the first recording with *Interrupted*
   (length *at least*, report *Interrupted (reload)*, *Recorded until …*) and a second one with *Continues*. Check the
   values just before the reload are there (probe P12).
2. `/reload` within the **5 s after** a fight ends: one complete recording, once.
3. `/logout` or *Exit game* during a fight, if the client lets you (write down if it refuses): after logging in,
   *Interrupted*, with the reason *login*, never still *Recording*.
4. `/logout` right after a fight, then log in: the fight is there and complete.
5. Close the game by force during a fight (end the process): what is left? Nothing, or an interrupted recording, is
   acceptable; a broken one or a Lua error is not.
6. Wait more than 2 minutes before fighting again after an interruption: no *Continues* badge.

## 6. The report after a wipe

The best test is a real one: a dungeon or raid wipe.

1. Open it: do the aggro losses, tank changes, deaths and times in Danger tell you what happened, in order? What is
   missing to understand it? (This is the exit criterion: note it in step 11.)
2. Is anything wrong or misleading compared with what you saw?
3. How long does *Building report…* stay for a long raid fight (probe P10)? Is there a **hitch** the moment you click it
   (the recording is read in one frame)?
4. Open and close it **in combat** while another fight is recorded: no error, no stutter.

## 7. The notice after a fight

1. Off (default): nothing appears after a fight.
2. On, window visible on the Monitor tab: about 5 s after the fight, the strip says *Fight recorded · …* with the number
   of threshold crossings and *Open report* for about 8 s; the click opens the Reports tab on that recording.
3. Window closed: the window does **not** open. A dropped or interrupted fight: no notice. Never a sound or a chat line.

## 8. Limits, deletion and the size of the file

1. *Keep at most* **5**: with more than five recordings, the oldest go **at once**. Fight again: still five, the newest
   first.
2. *Size limit* **0.5 MB** after some long fights: the oldest go until the estimate is under it; the fight in progress is
   never removed.
3. **Delete recording**: asks a second click within 8 s (wait longer: it disarms); gone after `/reload`.
4. **Delete all recordings** in the card: second click, the list is empty after `/reload`. During a fight the one being
   recorded stays.
5. After deleting all and logging out, search the saved-variables file for a companion's name: it is not there.
6. **File size.** After 10 or more fights (include a raid one if you can), compare the *about N KB* of the tab's header
   with the size of `AzerothThreatwarden.lua` after logging out. Write down both, the number of recordings and the
   longest fight. Does `/reload` or login feel slower with a full store?
7. `/atw debug` *Recording*: kept, pruned, damaged and set aside counts match what you did; damaged must be 0.

## 9. Readability and combat

1. The list and an open report at window scale 50 %, 100 % and 200 %, the smallest and largest UI scale, the narrowest
   and lowest window, three themes, English and Italian. Note texts cut or overlapping (badges and enemy name in a list
   row are the likeliest), a report too small under the list (it keeps three rows above), markers you cannot tell apart.
2. Scroll a raid report with many member cards: smooth?
3. In combat: show, hide, move, resize, switch tabs, open, close and delete reports, change the *Recording* card. No
   error, no blocked action.
4. Tooltips of the list rows and of the metrics read well and stay on screen.

## 10. Cost (probe P10)

In a party and in a raid, same fight, `/atwp watch off`.

1. With the window **closed**, in a fight: `/atw debug` shows the *Scheduler* ticks `threat` and `record` running and
   *Threat scanner* `active` (with recording off it is `idle`). Copy it: *record tick: … ms median, … ms max* and the
   `threat` line.
2. Compare frame rate and stutter with recording **on** and **off** (switch *Record fights*), window closed, at 2 and
   5 Hz recording rate.
3. Memory over a long session (`/atw debug` at the start and after 30 minutes of fights): it grows with the recordings
   and then stops at the limit.
4. The [budgets](../specs/SPEC-01-platform-architecture.md#51-il-costo-di-un-tick-del-monitor-misura-offline-di-m27)
   measured offline count instructions, not milliseconds: your numbers are the first real ones.

(M1–M4), as of M4.7. **Done**: met by the code and checked offline. **Open**: a development step still has work to do.
**Waiting for the owner**: only a run in the real client, or an owner's action, can close it.

## What to report if something does not work

| Symptom | What to send |
|---|---|
| Lua error or blocked action | the error text, what you did, `/atw debug` |
| A recording missing, doubled, split or merged wrongly | what you did and when, `/atw debug` *Recording* |
| A wrong number in a report | context, what you saw during the fight, a screenshot (names hidden), the metric's tooltip, `/atw debug` |
| Lost or damaged data after a reload | `/atw debug`; keep a copy of the file, do not attach it (it holds names) |
| Slowness, stutter or a large file | step 8.6 and 10 numbers, group size, other addons |
| Unreadable or cut text | screenshot (names hidden), window size, scale, language, theme |
