# M3 in-game check: alerts and the Threat Coach

The offline tests prove that the code agrees with our assumptions about the client; this checklist proves that the
client agrees with the code. It checks the exit criterion of [milestone M3](../roadmap/M3-alerts-coach.md) (*a DPS
nearing the tank gets one warning (not one per update), the cooldown holds, nothing goes to chat, and every Coach
message shows its reason*) and it is **not done yet**: nobody has run the addon on WoW Forever. Alerts and Coach were
written before the [M0 probe](../spike/README.md) was run, on assumptions.

**They stand on the monitor.** Alerts and Coach only read the monitor's states and values, so run the
[M2 checklist](M2.md) (with the probe) first or in the same sessions: a wrong state there makes a wrong alert here.
What the game's [manual page](../wiki/Alerts-and-Coach.md) describes is what you should see; the rules are in
[SPEC-04](../specs/SPEC-04-alerts-coach.md) (Italian).

Report the results with the [*In-game test report* form](https://github.com/Finland6738/azeroth-threatwarden/issues/new?template=ingame_test.yml)
(*Check*: for example `M3 step 2.3`) and note them in the table [at the end](#result). One report for everything that
passed is enough; open one per thing that did not.

**Silence is not always a bug.** With unreadable threat data there is nothing to alert about, and the Coach says why.
What must never happen: a message in any chat (yours included), the same alert repeated on every update, a sound or a
window you did not ask for, an invented number, a message that names a cause or an ability, or a Lua error.

## What you need

- WoW Forever; a **party** with a tank and a damage dealer, and if you can a **healer** (swap roles to see all three),
  a character with a **pet** (hunter), an enemy with many hit points (an elite) or a training dummy, a **dungeon**.
- On the PC, as in the [M2 checklist](M2.md#what-you-need): `v0.3.0` or later, or a development copy of an up-to-date
  `main`:

```bash
python -I -X utf8 tools/fetch_libs.py
```

```bash
python -I -X utf8 tools/install_addon.py
```

- Lua errors visible: `/console scriptErrors 1`, or BugSack.
- **Both switches on.** `/atw debug` *Features* must list `alerts` and `coach` after `on:`. From `v0.3.0` they are on.
  On an older copy of `main` they are off: after installing, open
  `<WoW folder>/_forever_/Interface/AddOns/AzerothThreatwarden/Core/Features.lua`, change `alerts = false` and
  `coach = false` to `true`, and restart the game. Edit only the installed copy (installing again undoes it); never
  commit that change.
- Settings at their defaults (*Reset settings*): alerts, message and visual effect on, **sound off**, cooldown **8 s**,
  *Alerts about others* **When I tank**, pet alert on, Threat Coach on.
- Keep the window **open on the Monitor tab** unless a step says otherwise: a message or a border effect that arrives
  while the window is closed is not kept for later. An alert from the very first reading of a fight, before the window
  opens by itself, is not shown: note it if it matters.

`/atw debug` has no player, guild or realm names. Screenshots show names: replace them with `<player>`.

## 1. Loading, the card and the demo

Out of combat, nothing targeted.

1. No Lua error and no chat message at login. The Monitor tab has the **strip** between the enemy's header and the list,
   reading *Advice appears during combat*.
2. `/atw settings`: the **Alerts** card is below *Monitor*, nothing overlaps or is cut, in English and in Italian.
   *Go to the Monitor card* scrolls there. The thresholds line matches the Monitor sliders.
3. `/atw demo` and watch two turns (40 s each). You are the damage dealer: you should see advice that changes (a rise
   with *about N s* and a confidence pill, then Warning, Danger, then reassurance), alerts that hold the strip for about
   4 seconds, and the **border** coloured **once** per alert, never flashing. No sound, even with *Sound* on.
4. Hover the strip during an alert and during advice: whole text, **Why:**, the kind of data, the confidence, since
   when, and *Demo*.
5. Set *Alerts about others* to **Always** and watch the rogue take the mob; back to **When I tank**.
6. Turn off, one at a time: *Message in the window* (the border still colours, and the pill at the top shows the
   state's icon), *Visual effect* (the strip still shows), *Show alerts* (no alert, the channels greyed, advice stays),
   *Threat Coach* (alerts stay), then both (the strip disappears and the list takes its place). All apply at once and
   survive `/reload`.

## 2. Damage dealer nearing the tank (exit criterion)

The tank pulls and holds a mob with many hit points; you hold back, then build threat slowly.

1. Crossing **Warning** (82 %): **one** alert, not one per update (*Threat N %: ease off, fewer casts or lower ranks*),
   with the border in the Warning colour; it gives way to advice (*Ease off: less damage, fewer casts or lower ranks*;
   for a healer *less healing*). A predicted alert (*you may reach Danger in about N s*) may come first.
2. Crossing **Danger** (91 %): one new alert at once (*stop damage for a moment*; for a healer *stop healing for a
   moment*), border in the Danger colour. Does easing off at 82 % and stopping at 91 % keep you off the aggro?
3. Hover around 82 % and then around 91 % for a minute, going up and down: count the alerts. The same alert must not
   come back within **8 s**.
4. Switch to a second mob and back within 8 s while above a threshold: no repeat. A fall never alerts.
5. Take the aggro: **one** alert *You have aggro*, and advice that the tank needs to take it back. The tank taunts: the
   alert and the advice go away without a new alert on the way down.
6. Every advice and alert has its reason in the tooltip, never says **why** something happened and never names an
   ability or a class (only the generic *lower ranks*); a tank is never told to stop. A prediction says *may* and *about* and shows *HIGH* or *MEDIUM*.
7. **Nothing in chat**: no line in any channel, party, raid or your own chat frame (except the answers of `/atw mute`).
8. How long after the real change (the percentage, the mob turning) does the alert arrive? Note it (probe P6).

## 3. Tank losing the margin and the mob

You are the tank, with the role **Tank** assigned.

1. Hold the mob with a comfortable margin: advice *You have enough margin according to current data*.
2. A damage dealer closes on you: at 82 % *Margin shrinking: name at N %*; at 91 % *name is very close to taking the
   mob*. The advice may say they *may overtake you in about N s*.
3. **Cooldown trap** (Q12): make the second go from Warning to Danger in **less than 8 s**. The Danger alert of the same
   kind stays silent, and an *other unit* alert about the second should come instead. Is that enough, or should the more
   severe one get through?
4. Let them take the mob: **one** alert *name has the mob*, advice with *The cause is not known* in the reason. Taunt it
   back.
5. Hold the mob while somebody has more threat (after a taunt): *Someone has more threat than you*.
6. **Two tanks** (if you have them): swapping the mob with a taunt gives no *lost* alert; advice says *Another tank has
   the mob*.
7. **Others**: with several damage dealers rising at once, at most one alert about others every few seconds. With
   *Never*, none.
8. Repeat 3.2 without assigned roles: what happens to your role and the messages (probe P9)?

## 4. Healer

Healer role, in the group.

1. Target the **tank**: the monitor follows their enemy, and the strip works on it.
2. Build threat by healing until Warning, Danger and, if safe, aggro: alerts as in step 2, advice in the healer's words
   (*the mob may turn to you*, *The mob is on you*).
3. Do the messages arrive in time and help while you watch health bars? Is anything distracting?

## 5. Hunter's pet

1. **Solo** with the pet holding the mob: advice relative to the pet, no *pet* alert. Pull the mob off the pet: **one**
   *You have aggro*.
2. **In a group with a tank**: the pet takes the mob from the tank: **one** alert *Your pet has the mob*. With *Your pet
   takes the mob* off, none.
3. Solo **without** a pet, nobody else on the list: the Coach stays **silent** (empty strip), by design.

## 6. State only and unknown data

In a dungeon, a raid or wherever the monitor says *State only* or *Unavailable* (M2 checklist, step 5).

1. **State only**: alerts and advice without numbers or predictions (*Your threat is above the tank's*, *Your margin is
   very small*), reason *From the game's threat state*. No alert at a percentage threshold.
2. **Unavailable**: no alert; in combat the strip says **Not enough data for advice**, and the tooltip says why
   (if you are not on the list at all, the strip stays empty: write down which you saw).
3. Mixed rows, hidden names (*Party 2*) and hidden roles: no empty name, no wrong role's words.
4. The first seconds of a fight: *Not enough data for advice* while the trend is not ready is fine; note if it stays long.
5. A tank nobody can identify (*Unknown tank*): no aggro or lost alert.

## 7. Mute and the key

1. In combat, `/atw mute`: *Alerts muted until the end of this fight.*, the strip says so and shows the struck bell; no
   alert, border or sound; advice stays. Typed again: *Alerts back on.* The mute ends by itself when the fight ends.
2. Out of combat it says *…the next fight*, covers it and ends with it. A `/reload` removes it.
3. With *Show alerts* off, it answers *Alerts are off in Settings.*
4. **Key**: in the game's key bindings, **Azeroth Threatwarden → Mute alerts** exists (addons' category) with no key.
   Bind one: it does the same as the command, in and out of combat, with no blocked action. Is the name in your
   language? Does the binding survive a restart?
5. Repeat 1 in Italian.
6. **One unit**: right-click another row, **Mute alerts about this unit** (checked once chosen, tooltip says *muted*):
   no alert about it; again to unmute. Your own row has no menu. The menu opens where expected and closes with a click
   elsewhere, `Esc` and a tab change. A `/reload` forgets it.

## 8. The sound

*Sound* on in the card.

1. A **Danger** or aggro alert plays one game sound; **Warning** never does. Two severe alerts close together give one.
2. **Do you hear it?** Is it recognisable, too loud or annoying, and does it follow the effects volume? If silent, copy
   `/atw debug` *Alerts and Coach* (line `ui.PlaySound`): kit **8959** may not exist on Forever.
3. With the window **closed** and then on the Settings tab, the sound still plays in combat. Muted, or with *Sound* off:
   silence, and with the window closed `/atw debug` *Threat scanner* is `idle`.

## 9. The game's own threat warning

1. Turn the game's own threat warning **on** in the game options (note the option's name and what it shows or plays).
2. `/atw settings`, or `/reload`: does the Alerts card show the note that you may get two warnings? Off: no note.
   Copy the `threat.WarningEnabled` line of `/atw debug` in both cases.
3. In a fight with both on: is the doubling annoying? The addon must never change the game's setting.

## 10. Readability and cost in combat

1. Read the strip while fighting: at window scale 50 %, 100 % and 200 %, a narrow and a very low window (the list's
   header hides, the strip stays), the three themes, Italian. Which messages are cut with *…* (Italian ones are likely at
   the narrowest width)? Is the 4 s long enough?
2. The border effect: noticeable without being distracting? Also with *Animations* off (colour for 1.5 s, no fade).
3. Colour is never the only sign: every state has its icon and word.
4. No stutter or blocked action in a busy fight; copy `/atw debug` *Alerts and Coach* at the end (alerts raised, in
   cooldown, dropped).

## What to report if something does not work

| Symptom | What to send |
|---|---|
| Lua error or blocked action | the error text, what you did, `/atw debug` |
| Repeated, missing or wrong alert or advice | context and role, the percentages and states in order, the strip's tooltip, `/atw debug` right after |
| No sound | `/atw debug` *Alerts and Coach*, your sound settings |
| Anything in chat | the exact line, with names replaced |
| Unreadable or cut strip | screenshot (names hidden), window size, scale, language, theme |
