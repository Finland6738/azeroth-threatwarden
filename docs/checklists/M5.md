# M5 in-game check: Threat Timeline and Threat Replay

The offline tests prove that the code agrees with our assumptions about the client; this checklist proves that the
client agrees with the code. It checks the exit criterion of [milestone M5](../roadmap/M5-timeline-replay.md) (*a
recorded fight opens as a timeline with its events; play, pause, seek and step work; estimates and gaps are visibly
different from detected samples*). It is **not done yet**: nobody has run the addon on WoW Forever. The timeline and
the replay were written before the [M0 probe](../spike/README.md) was run, on assumptions.

**They stand on the recording.** The timeline and the replay only read a recorded session, so a wrong value there is a
wrong picture here. Run the probe and the [M4 checklist](M4.md) first or in the same sessions; the recordings made there
are the ones to open here. The [manual page](../wiki/Threat-Replay.md) describes what you should see; the rules are in
[SPEC-05 §4–§5](../specs/SPEC-05-recording-report-replay.md#4-threat-timeline-datatimelinelua-uitimelinelua-m52m53)
(Italian).

Report the results with the [*In-game test report* form](https://github.com/Finland6738/azeroth-threatwarden/issues/new?template=ingame_test.yml)
(*Check*: for example `M5 step 3.2`) and note them in the table [at the end](#result). One report for everything that
passed is enough; open one per thing that did not.

**A gap is not always a bug.** A hatched band, *State only*, *Nothing readable* or *No reading since …* with a reason is
the right answer when the recording holds nothing there. What must never happen: a line across a gap, an invented
number, an old number shown as current, a text that names a cause or an ability, a window that opens by itself, a
message in any chat, or a Lua error.

## What you need

- WoW Forever; a **party** with a tank and a damage dealer, a **dungeon** (trash and a boss) and a **raid** if you can;
  an enemy with many hit points or a training dummy.
- On the PC, as in the [M2 checklist](M2.md#what-you-need), a development copy of an up-to-date `main` (or the released
  `v0.5.0`).
- Lua errors visible: `/console scriptErrors 1`, or BugSack.
- **Back up your saved data first**, with the game closed. Copy both files somewhere else:
  - `<WoW folder>/_forever_/WTF/Account/<account>/SavedVariables/AzerothThreatwarden.lua` (settings and recordings);
  - `<WoW folder>/_forever_/WTF/Account/<account>/<realm>/<character>/SavedVariables/AzerothThreatwarden.lua` (window
    place and size), for each character you use.

  A copy of `main` (like `v0.5.0`) raises the character data to schema 2: if you go back to `v0.4.0` afterwards, that version shows it
  **read-only** until you restore your copy. The files hold the names of your companions: do not attach them to an issue.

Then install:

```bash
python -I -X utf8 tools/fetch_libs.py
```

```bash
python -I -X utf8 tools/install_addon.py
```

- **Switches**: since `v0.5.0` timeline and replay are on; nothing to edit. `/atw debug` *Features* must list
  `timeline` and `replay` after `on:`, and the *Timeline and replay* section must say
  `switches: timeline on, replay on`.
- Settings at their defaults (*Reset settings*): *Record fights* on, *When* **In a group or instance**, rate **2 Hz**.
  Recordings made before (M4) open too.

`/atw debug` (section **Timeline and replay**) has counts and times only, no names, so its text is safe to paste.
Screenshots show names: replace them with `<player>`.

## 1. Login and the command

1. No Lua error and no chat message at login; nothing opens by itself.
2. `/atw replay` with no recordings says *No recordings to replay yet.*; with some, `/atw replay 99` says *No
   recording number 99* and how many there are; `/atw help` lists `replay`.

## 2. A fight in a party: the timeline (exit criterion)

Record a fight of one to three minutes: the tank pulls, you go above Warning and Danger, take the aggro once, the tank
taunts back, somebody dies if you can. Wait 5 s after it ends.

1. Open its report: below the first card, *Building timeline…* then the **Timeline** card with your line, who held the
   enemy, and event icons. A report opens as quickly as before.
2. Check against what you saw: the order and the times of the events; the thresholds at 82 % and 91 % and the 100 % line;
   the tank at 100 % while holding the enemy; changes of target.
3. Hold the mouse over lines, points, gaps and icons: each says its category and, for a gap, the reason; no number
   appears between two readings. Icons close together show their number and list the events.
4. Click the card: the replay opens at that time. Close it; open it with the **Replay** button (from the start) and with
   `/atw replay` (the latest finished fight) and `/atw replay 2`.
5. During your first recorded fight (nothing else in the list), `/atw replay` says it is still being recorded;
   `/atw replay 1` during any recorded fight shows the same text. If you can reach a report of the recording in
   progress, its *Replay* button is off and its tooltip says why.

## 3. Play, pause, seek, step, jump (exit criterion)

In the replay, out of combat.

1. **Play** (button): the cursor moves, the detail follows, the event list highlights the last event passed; **Pause**
   stops it. At 1×, 2×, 4× and 8× it moves that fast (time 10 s against your clock). At the end it stops; Play again
   starts from the beginning of the view.
2. Click the chart: the cursor goes there. **Step** forwards and backwards moves to the next recorded reading or event;
   **Previous / next event** and **start / end** work.
3. `Shift` + `Left` / `Right` jumps 5 s.
4. **Drag** on the chart: the interval is selected, hovering inside shows its summary; **right click** clears it. *Zoom
   to selection*, *Whole fight* and the mouse wheel over the chart zoom; seeking outside the view scrolls it.
5. Click a name in the detail to show or hide its line (at most six).
6. **Event focus**: click an event, with each filter (*Key events*, *Alerts & tips*, *All*): the cursor goes there, the
   playback pauses, 5 s before and after are selected, the detail lists the readings around it and **Missing data in
   ±5 s** (or *No missing data in this window*). Is the selection summary shown while hovering in that window what you
   want, or would you rather see the point under the pointer? Write it down.
7. An old reading shows as *~61.0 % · 1.8 s before*; a missing one says why and since when.
8. The mouse wheel scrolls the event list and the detail; after you scroll the list, it stays put for 3 s during
   playback.

## 4. Dashed lines and gaps (Q20)

1. Find (or make) gaps in one fight: change target, let the enemy die and pull again within 5 s, leave the threat list
   (move far away, or die), `/atw demo` is **not** recorded. Each is a hatched or *Out of combat* band with its reason,
   and no line crosses it.
2. **Dashed lines** (estimates) are rare at the defaults: in a raid with *Units per read* lowered in the *Monitor* card of
   Settings below the group size, they should appear. Can you tell dashed, solid and gap apart at a glance, in every theme?
3. A chart where nothing can be known never looks like 0 %: it has the note instead.

## 5. Dungeon and raid: the three outcomes and a mixed fight (probe P3)

Open the recordings of the M4 checklist, step 4, on trash and on a boss in a **dungeon**, then a **raid**. Note which
outcome each context gives.

1. **Values**: lines with percentages, holder, states.
2. **State only**: the note *Percentages were not available…*, state lanes and who held the enemy, no percentage
   anywhere; the detail says the situation in words.
3. **Nothing readable**: the note with the reason, only the events; the window does not look broken.
4. **Mixed** (the mode changes during the fight): lines in the readable stretches, a band *State only from …* or
   *Nothing readable from …* elsewhere, never a line across the change.
5. Hidden names show as *Party 2*, *Raid 13* or *Pet*, never empty.
6. A big raid fight: how long does *Building timeline…* stay, and is there a hitch when you click? Copy `/atw debug`
   (*model: … built in … ms over … frames*, *pools: …*) (probe P10).

## 6. Interrupted and truncated recordings

1. `/reload` in the middle of a fight, keep fighting, finish. The first recording has the badge *Interrupted*, ends at
   its last record with a cross marker, and nothing is drawn after; **Continues in…** and **Continued from…** open the
   other one.
2. Logout during a fight, if the client allows it: the same.
3. A **truncated** recording needs the 30 000-row limit (about 6 minutes with 40 units at 2 Hz; a long raid fight) or
   6 000 events: the band *Samples stopped: limit reached* and, in the detail, *Samples stopped at …*; events continue
   after it. If you cannot reach it, write *skipped*.
4. A **damaged** recording is not something to make on purpose; if you ever have one, it must say so and have no replay.

## 7. Combat while the replay is open (Q18)

1. Play, then start a fight: the playback **pauses**, *Play* is off with *Playback pauses during combat*; seeking,
   picking events and reading the detail still work.
2. The window does not open, close or move by itself; combat keys and abilities work as usual with the pointer over it.
3. After the fight, *Play* works again; the playback does not restart on its own.
4. Open the replay **during** combat (button, card, command), resize it, switch filters, close it: no error, no blocked
   action.
5. **Delete** the recording that the replay shows: the window says *This recording was deleted.*

Is pausing the right default, or should playback go on in combat?

## 8. Keys and Esc (Q19)

Out of combat.

1. With the pointer **over the window**: Space, Left, Right, `Shift` + Left / Right, Up, Down, Home, End, 1–4 do what
   their tooltips say.
2. With the pointer **outside** it, the same keys do what the game binds to them (jump, action bars, movement).
3. Over the window, every other key, any key with `Ctrl` or `Alt`, and `Shift` with other keys still reaches the game
   (try `Enter` for chat, `M` for the map, your bindings). Typing in a chat box is not disturbed.
4. Enter combat with the pointer over the window: the keys belong to the game at once, and come back after the fight.
5. `Esc` closes the replay, in combat too, and does not open the game menu in the same press. With both windows open,
   what does `Esc` close first?
6. No "action blocked" message mentions the addon. Write down any binding that the window swallows.

## 9. Lines, regions, frame rate and colours

1. Look at the lines: continuous, even thickness (your own line thicker), clean edges at UI scale 0.64, 0.75, 0.9 and
   window scale 50 %, 100 %, 200 %. Dashes (4 px) read as dashes; the hatched gap texture is not stretched into
   something else.
2. The cursor and the tooltip are where the pointer is, at every UI and window scale (a shift means the pointer position
   is scaled wrong).
3. A long raid recording: frame rate with the replay open, while hovering, playing at 8×, dragging a selection,
   resizing and zooming. Copy `/atw debug` *pools* (*columns widened* means a cap was reached) and *replay tick*.
4. In each of the **three themes** (Nebula, Warden, Aurora): can you tell the six series apart, from each other, from
   the state colours and from the background? The player's blue against *Unknown* in Aurora is the closest pair.
5. A client that draws no lines would show dots and bars, and the tooltip says so: report it if you see it.
6. Do names on the chart overlap the 100 % line or each other in the report's card?

## 10. Window sizes, scales and memory

1. **The main window** (fixed after `v0.4.0`): resize it from the corner on the **Reports** tab, with and without a
   report open, and on **Settings**: **no Lua error**, and the content follows while you drag.
2. The replay at the smallest size (**640 × 420**: the zoom buttons become icons), under 760 points wide (one panel with
   *Events · Details*), the default and the largest; window scale 50 % to 200 %, English and Italian. Note texts cut or
   overlapping, buttons or the six playback icons you cannot read.
3. Is the event list and the detail comfortable to read? The chart in the report's card too?
4. Move and resize the replay, change speed, `/reload` and log out and in: place, size and speed are kept, per
   character. `/atw reset` resets the main window only (Q21): right?
5. It stays on screen when dragged to the edges. Change theme while it is open: it repaints.
6. Open and close it twenty times, on different recordings: no growing delay; `/atw debug` memory and *pools* stay
   about the same after the first openings.

## What to report if something does not work

| Symptom | What to send |
|---|---|
| Lua error or blocked action | the error text, what you did, `/atw debug` |
| A wrong picture (line across a gap, wrong time, missing event) | context, what you saw during the fight, a screenshot (names hidden), the tooltip text, `/atw debug` |
| Keys doing the wrong thing | the key, where the pointer was, in or out of combat, what the game binds to it |
| Slowness or stutter | steps 5.6 and 9.3 numbers, group size, length of the fight, other addons |
| Unreadable or cut text, colours too close | screenshot (names hidden), window size, scale, language, theme |
